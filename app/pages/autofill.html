Stop typing the same application again

DreamJob Autofill is a free browser extension for Chrome and Edge. Save your details once. On any application form, click Fill with DreamJob or press Alt+Shift+F, and your name, contact details, address, links, work history, education, common answers and résumé go into the right fields.

Download the extension (v{{VERSION}})

Works best on

Workday (including multi-step applications), Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workable, iCIMS, SuccessFactors, Taleo, Recruitee, Breezy, Personio, BambooHR and Jobvite. On other sites, open the extension and choose Fill this page.

Install in 1 minute

  1. Download the zip above and unzip it. You get a folder named dreamjob-autofill.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome (or edge://extensions in Edge).
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right in Chrome, left side in Edge).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the dreamjob-autofill folder.
  5. Your profile page opens. Choose your résumé, enter this site's address (this site) and click Import details from résumé. Check the details and click Save profile.
  6. Pin the extension from the puzzle-piece menu so it is one click away.

How it behaves

  • It only fills empty fields, unless you tick “Replace fields that already have a value”.
  • It never clicks Submit, Next or consent boxes. Filled fields get a green outline so you can review them.
  • On Workday, fill each step as it appears. Missing work experience and education blocks are added for you.
  • Questions it doesn't know are left empty. Add your own answers in the profile, for example “notice period” → “30 days”.

Privacy

Your profile and résumé are stored only in your browser. The extension only reads a page when you ask it to fill one. Details are in the privacy policy.
